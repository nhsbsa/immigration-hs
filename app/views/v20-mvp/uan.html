Application What's the applicant's Unique Application Number? (optional)

Providing your Unique Application Number (UAN) helps us to assess your eligibility more effectively.

! Warning The UAN you provide must be associated with the visa you held during the period you're claiming for
Where to find your Unique Application Number
If you've applied for a visa while in the UK

You can find your UAN on your application successful email (labelled as 'Ref:'). Your UAN is also available from your UKVI online account (opens in new tab).

For visa applications from outside the UK

The UAN could be given on your visa appointment document. It could also be on your UKVI online, if you have linked the BRP for your visa to an eVisa.

Ensure that you have the correct number before continuing with this application.
For example, 1234-5678-9098-7654 or 1234-5678-9098-7654/00
