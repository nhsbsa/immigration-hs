Application What's the applicant's Unique Application Number? (optional)

Providing your Unique Application Number (UAN) helps us to assess your eligibility for an IHS reimbursement more effectively.

! Warning The UAN you provide must be associated with the visa you held during the period you're claiming for.
Where to find your UAN
If you've applied for a visa while in the UK

You can find your UAN on your application successful email (it could be labelled a 'Unique Application Number' or 'Ref:'). Your UAN is also available from your UKVI online account.

For visa applications from outside the UK

Your UAN could be given on your completed visa application (if you downloaded it at the time of submission). Your UAN is also available from your UKVI online account.

eVisas

Use the UAN associated with the visa for the period you are claiming for.

If you have a biometric residence permit (BRP)

If you have linked your BRP to an eVisa, use the UAN associated with the visa for the period you are claiming for. Do not use the UAN provided when you first set up the eVisa.

UKVI online

Access your UKVI online account (opens in new tab) .

For example, 1234-5678-9098-7654 or 1234-5678-9098-7654/00
