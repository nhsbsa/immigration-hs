Application What's the applicant's Unique Application Number (UAN)?

A UAN is a 16-digit reference number that is used to track a person's visa status.

Providing a UAN helps us assess the applicant's eligibility for an IHS reimbursement more effectively.

! Warning The UAN you provide must be associated with the visa the applicant held during the period they're claiming for.
Where to find the UAN
If the visa application was made while the applicant was in the UK

The UAN can be found on the payment confirmation email (labelled as 'Ref') or application successful email (labelled as 'Unique Application Number'). The UAN is also available on a UKVI account.

If the visa application was made while the applicant was outside the UK

The UAN can be found on the completed visa application (if you downloaded it at the time of submission).

If the applicant is claiming for a period when they held biometric residence permit (BRP)

The BRP will have been converted to an eVisa. You should use the UAN the applicant received when they first made an IHS payment.

Finding the UAN on a UKVI account

Login to the UKVI online account (opens in new tab) .The UAN is in the 'Your forms and applications' section and is labelled as 'Unique Application Number (UAN)'.

You'll need the applicant's identity documents or UKVI customer number to login to their UKVI account.

Applicants without an account can set up a UKVI account online (opens in new tab).

If the applicant previously had a biometric residence permit (BRP) that was converted to an eVisa, they might not have a UAN. In this case, leave the UAN fields empty and select 'Continue' to resume the application.

For example, 1234-5678-9098-7654 or 1234-5678-9098-7654/00
