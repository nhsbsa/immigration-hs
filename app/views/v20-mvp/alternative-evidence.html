If you cannot provide a share code for the visa you held during the period you're claiming for, you must provide alternative evidence.

Examples of accepted evidence are:

  • visa vignette (sticker in passport)
  • Biometric Residence Permit (BRP)
  • Home Office grant letter or email
  • Home Office Immigration status document

Evidence should include the applicant's:

  • name
  • visa start date
  • visa expiry date

Files

Files must:

  • be clear and readable
  • be in JPG, JPEG, BMP, PNG or PDF format
  • be smaller than 2MB each
  • not be password protected
