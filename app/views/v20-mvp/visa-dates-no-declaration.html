Application What are the dates of the visa associated with this IHS claim?

These are the start and end dates of the visa the applicant held during the period they're claiming for.

! Warning If the applicant's IHS claim dates are outside the visa dates you provide, their claim will be rejected.
Where to find the applicant's visa dates
For a current visa, dates can be found on the applicant's eVisa document. This is available online through their UKVI account (opens in new tab).

For expired visas, dates can be found on:

  • the Home Office decision letter (grant letter)
  • the visa vignette (sticker) in the passport, if one was issued for that visa
  • the previous Biometric Residence Permit (BRP), if the visa was issued before the move to eVisas
  • an official Home Office email or PDF confirming that the visa was approved

Visa start date

This is the date the visa was approved, for example, 31 6 2022

Visa end date

This is the date the visa expires, for example, 31 12 2022
